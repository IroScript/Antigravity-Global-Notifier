The **Storage Object User** role (`roles/storage.objectUser`) in Google Cloud provides permissions to **create, read, update, and delete objects** and multipart uploads within Cloud Storage[1][2].

### Key Details:
*   **Purpose:** This role is typically used to grant principals the ability to manage objects (files) within a bucket or managed folder without granting them full control over the bucket itself (such as the ability to delete the bucket or change its configuration)[2][3].
*   **Permissions:** It includes permissions to interact with objects and their metadata[2]. It does **not** include the `storage.buckets.list` permission, meaning users with only this role may not be able to list buckets in the Google Cloud console unless they are granted additional permissions or navigate directly to a known bucket path[4][5].
*   **Common Use Cases:**
    *   Allowing service accounts or users to upload/remove objects from a specific managed folder[3].
    *   Granting read/write access to data for applications or pipelines[4][6].

For a complete and current list of all individual permissions included in this role, you can consult the [official Google Cloud IAM roles documentation](https://cloud.google.com/iam/docs/understanding-roles) or the Google Cloud console's "Roles" page[2][7].
