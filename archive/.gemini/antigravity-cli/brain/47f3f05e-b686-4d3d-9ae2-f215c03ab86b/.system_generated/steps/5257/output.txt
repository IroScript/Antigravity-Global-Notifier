### The Matcher
For tool-specific events (`PreToolUse`, `PostToolUse`), you must wrap the

*   `"matcher": "run_command\|view_file"`: Matches either tool.
*   `"matcher": "browser_.*"`: Matches any tool starting with `browser_`.
