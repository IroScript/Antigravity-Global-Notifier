The search for ""Google Drive" "Contributor" delete files "Shared Drive"" returned the following summary:
In Google Shared Drives, a user with the **Contributor** role **cannot delete files or move them to the trash**[1][2][3].

### Summary of Contributor Permissions
*   **What they can do:** Contributors are intended to be content creators; they can add, edit, and update files within the Shared Drive[1][4].
*   **What they cannot do:** They do not have permission to delete files, move files to the trash, or move files/folders within the Shared Drive[1][5][3].
*   **Important Limitation:** While Contributors cannot delete files, they **can** often upload a new file with the exact same name as an existing one, which may effectively overwrite or replace the original content without a notification being sent to the owner or admin[6][7][8].

### Deletion Permissions by Role
If you need to manage who can delete content, it is helpful to understand the hierarchy of roles in a Shared Drive:

| Role | Can Delete/Trash Files? |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Manager** | Yes[1][9] |
| **Content Manager** | Yes[1][10][11] |
| **Contributor** | **No**[1][2] |
| **Commenter** | No[1][3] |
| **Viewer** | No[1][3] |

If you are concerned about accidental deletions, assigning the **Contributor** role (or lower) is the recommended way to restrict those specific privileges[12][10][13].
